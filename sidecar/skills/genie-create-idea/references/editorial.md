Primary parent communication addresses parents/guardians of grades 4–6; students, teachers, and schools are secondary audiences. Use a calm, warm, practical Indonesian teacher's voice. Explain a useful learning action rather than pressuring parents or promising scores.

4. Tips Belajar untuk Siswa dan Orang Tua
